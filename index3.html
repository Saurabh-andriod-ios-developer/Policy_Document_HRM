Gathero Account Deletion

At Gathero, we respect your privacy and give you the option to delete your account and request deletion of your associated personal data.

1. How to Delete Your Account

To delete your account from the Gathero app, follow these steps:

  1. Open the Gathero app and log in to your account.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Follow the on-screen instructions to confirm your request.

Account deletion may be irreversible. Please review any confirmation message shown in the app before proceeding.

2. Cannot Access the App?

If you cannot access Gathero or have uninstalled the app, you can contact us to request account deletion.

Email: saurabh.barapatre@pletratech.com

Please use the subject line Gathero Account Deletion Request. Include the email address or other account identifier associated with your Gathero account so we can verify your request. Do not send your password.

3. What Data Is Deleted?

When your account deletion is completed, personal data associated with your account will be deleted, subject to applicable legal, security, and operational requirements.

Depending on the data stored by Gathero, this may include your profile information, account details, and other personal data associated with your account.

4. Data Retention

Certain records may need to be retained for a limited period where required by law, for legitimate security purposes, or to resolve disputes and prevent fraud. Any retained data will be handled according to applicable requirements and Gathero's Privacy Policy.
