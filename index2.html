Privacy Policy - Gathero

Effective Date: October 12, 2026

Last Updated: October 12, 2026

Gathero is an event-planning application that allows users to discover events, create events, connect with other users, and communicate through event-related group chats. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Name and account details.
  • Email address and phone number, where provided.
  • Profile information and selected city.
  • Event details, including descriptions, dates, and venues.
  • Messages sent through group chats.
  • Coin balances, purchase records, and transaction references, where applicable.
  • Technical and diagnostic information, where collected.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage accounts.
  • To authenticate users and protect account security.
  • To discover and display public events.
  • To allow users to create and participate in events.
  • To provide group chat and communication features.
  • To manage coin purchases and transactions, where applicable.
  • To troubleshoot issues and improve app functionality.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.

3. Events and User-Generated Content

Event information submitted for public listings may be visible to other users. Messages and other content may be visible to members of the relevant group, depending on the feature. Users should avoid sharing sensitive personal information in public events or group chats.

4. Location Information

Gathero may use the city selected by a user to display relevant events. If precise device location is collected by a feature, the app will request the necessary permission where required. Location information is handled according to the app's actual functionality and permissions.

5. Payments and Virtual Coins

Gathero may offer virtual coins or paid features. Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers, such as Razorpay, where applicable. We may receive payment status, transaction references, and purchase records needed to manage transactions. Payment providers handle payment information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We may use service providers for hosting, databases, authentication, payment processing, and other app functionality. Information may be shared as necessary to provide these services, comply with applicable laws, protect users, and prevent fraud.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

7. Data Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain account and transaction records, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

9. Account and Data Deletion

Users may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information. If account deletion is available within the app, users may also use that feature. Some information may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes.

10. Children's Privacy

Gathero is not intended for children who are below the minimum age permitted to use the service under applicable law. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information where prohibited by law.

11. International Data Transfers

Information may be processed in countries where our service providers operate. Where required, we take appropriate steps to protect personal information during international transfers.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page along with the updated date.
